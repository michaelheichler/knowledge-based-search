## Overview

This chapter covers the major people search engines available for free or partially free use, supplementary personal data sources (rebate trackers, utility portals, gift registries, and voter records), and tools for finding resumes and locating deceased individuals. The focus is on U.S. targets, though a few international options are noted. The recommended order of use runs from most to least productive based on the author's investigative experience.

## People Search Engines

People search engines aggregate publicly available data and present it in a summary format, often drawing on the same underlying databases as general search engines. All of the following provide free baseline searches, though each also promotes paid upgrades that the author consistently advises against purchasing until free options are exhausted. Many of these sites now block IP addresses associated with VPNs because competing sites use those addresses for bulk scraping.

**True People Search** (truepeoplesearch.com) is ranked first for comprehensive, consistent, free results. A typical report includes current address, previous addresses, phone numbers including cellular, email addresses, relatives, and associates. Direct URL: `https://www.truepeoplesearch.com/results?name=michael%20bazzell`.

**Fast People Search** (fastpeoplesearch.com) draws on what appears to be the same database but is valuable because many people who opted out of True People Search never replicated the removal here. Direct URL: `https://www.fastpeoplesearch.com/name/michael-bazzell`.

**Nuwber** (nuwber.com) presents results organized by location. Each profile typically includes full name, age range, home address, telephone number, and neighbors. Direct URL: `https://nuwber.com/search?name=michael-bazzell`.

**Open Data USA** (usa-official.com) returns full name, current and previous home addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses, possible relatives, and age. The author found email addresses here not present on any other site. Direct URL: `https://usa-official.com/Michael-Bazzell`.

**Family Tree Now** (familytreenow.com) specializes in connecting a person to relatives. It does not display home addresses but provides the target's age and an extensive list of family members. Direct URL: `https://www.familytreenow.com/search/genealogy/results?first=Michael&last=Bazzell`.

**Cyber Background Checks** (cyberbackgroundchecks.com) is suspected to share a parent company with True People Search and Fast People Search, but occasionally surfaces unique data. Direct URL: `https://www.cyberbackgroundchecks.com/people/michael-bazzell`.

**Intelius** (intelius.com) is a premium service. No information is displayed for free, but the preview screen before payment shows exact age, possible aliases, cities lived in, previous employers, universities attended, relatives, spouse, and often the wife's maiden name. The author does not recommend purchasing the full report. Direct URL: `https://www.intelius.com/people-search/Michael-Bazzell`.

**Radaris** (radaris.com) follows a similar business model to Intelius. The free "Full Profile" view often includes middle name, age, current and previous address, landline telephone number, and links to social networks. Direct URL: `https://radaris.com/p/Michael/Bazzell/`.

**Spytox** (spytox.com) provides limited free data (typically city, state, and phone only). Direct URL: `https://www.spytox.com/michael-bazzell`.

**Search People Free** (searchpeoplefree.com) shares apparent origins with True People Search and Fast People Search, but occasionally holds records absent from the others. Email addresses in results are hyperlinks that sometimes connect to additional names. Direct URL: `https://www.searchpeoplefree.com/find/michael-bazzell`.

**That's Them** (thatsthem.com) is notable for data not freely available elsewhere. The "Name and Address" search option can return age range, cell and landline phone numbers, full address, religion, financial details, home IP address, and associated email addresses. The author confirmed it returned the exact VIN number of a previous vehicle. Religion and financial fields are noted as unreliable. Direct URL: `https://thatsthem.com/name/Michael-Bazzell`.

**Spokeo** (spokeo.com) is the most widely recognized people search service. The free version shows full name, gender, age, and previous cities and states of residence but no longer displays the current address. The animated loading display is theatrical and not a real-time data query. A static URL bypasses the animation: `https://www.spokeo.com/Michael-Bazzell?loaded=1`.

**Advanced Background Checks** (advancedbackgroundchecks.com) presents data similar to Intelius, but most of the archive is accessible without payment. Results that appear redacted on the main page open in full on a separate page, typically including home address, landline telephone, age, and relatives. The "See full info" button reveals previous addresses, additional phone numbers, and aliases. Direct URL: `https://www.advancedbackgroundchecks.com/names/michael-bazzell`.

**Yasni** (yasni.com) is a German service with an international strength. It returns news articles, websites, and social network profiles associated with a name. Searching by location narrows results to a specific target. It does not list the United States as a filter option, making it most useful for non-U.S. subjects. Direct URL: `https://www.yasni.com/jason+edison/check+people`.

**Zaba Search** (zabasearch.com) has one useful free option among several links that forward to paid Intelius pages. Providing a name and state returns a page with full name, date of birth, address, and phone number, often including unlisted numbers. The first results page is the only reliable output. Direct URL: `https://www.zabasearch.com/people/michael+bazzell/`.

**People Search Now** (peoplesearchnow.com) appears to share a parent company and database with True People Search. Useful when a target has removed profiles from related sites. Direct URL: `https://www.peoplesearchnow.com/person/michael-bazzell`.

**WebMii** (webmii.com) focuses on social network presence. It rarely returns addresses or phone numbers but has surfaced online images not found elsewhere. Direct URL: `http://webmii.com/people?n="Michael%20Bazzell"`.

**Social Searcher** (social-searcher.com) focuses on social media presence and mentions within posts. Direct URL: `https://www.social-searcher.com/search-users/?q6=Michael+Bazzell`.

**Truth Finder** (truthfinder.com) presents data similar to other services but is marred by fake progress meters that can extend a search to over 14 minutes. Submitting a direct URL bypasses the delay: `https://www.truthfinder.com/results/?firstName=Michael&lastName=Bazzell&state=ALL`.

**People By Name** (peoplebyname.com) is misleadingly named because its standard interface only accepts phone numbers. A specific URL structure retrieves name-based profiles: `http://www.peoplebyname.com/people/Bazzell/Michael`.

**White Pages** (whitepages.com) performs reverse name or address searches and returns known residents and neighbors. Data is drawn from public information and is rarely complete. Direct URL: `https://www.whitepages.com/name/Michael-Bazzell`.

**Clustr Maps** (clustrmaps.com) is similar to the above services. Direct URL: `https://clustrmaps.com/persons/Michael-Bazzell`.

**Public Records** (publicrecords.directory) draws on voter registration databases, campaign contribution disclosures, and vehicle registration data. These categories do not appear consistently. No direct URL submission is available.

**Rocket Reach** (rocketreach.co) focuses on professional data scraped from LinkedIn and online resumes rather than home addresses. The site requires an account, but running a `site:rocketreach.co "target name"` query in Google can surface profiles viewable without login. Example output includes location, work history, education, partially redacted email addresses, and partially redacted phone numbers.

**Official USA** (officialusa.com) typically returns full name, home address, email address, phone number, and home sale records. Queries are submitted manually through the "Search for all governments" field.

**Addresses** (addresses.com) draws on public datasets and applies fewer redactions than many premium sites. Full phone numbers, addresses, and family connections are commonly visible. Direct URL: `https://www.addresses.com/people/michael-bazzell`.

**ID Crawl** (idcrawl.com) attempts to build a full online profile from a name. Results vary. Direct URL: `https://www.idcrawl.com/michael-bazzell`.

**Classmates** (classmates.com) requires a free account (which may contain fictitious information) and a completed profile to access full features. Searching by name, and optionally by school attended, can return the school and years of attendance. The site also hosts scanned yearbooks that must be browsed page by page. This content is likely to be unique from standard internet searches.

## IntelTechniques Names Tool

The author created a custom tool at `https://inteltechniques.com/tools/Name.html` (also available as an offline download) that submits a name to all supported people search sites simultaneously, opening results in multiple tabs. Middle names and initials are intentionally excluded from the query fields because many people search sites do not include middle names, and adding one risks omitting results.

## Rebate Tracking

Retail rebate portals can reveal shopping habits, purchase dates, and home address details. The Menards rebate tracker at `https://rebateinternational.com/RebateInternational/tracking.do` accepts a first initial, last name, numeric portion of an address, and postal code, returning purchased items, purchase dates, rebate amounts, and payment details. The result is accessible through a static, unauthenticated URL (example: `https://rebateinternational.com/RebateInternational/trackResults/M/Smith/1212/77089`), making it vulnerable to brute-force enumeration. The Lowe's rebate portal at `https://lowes-rebates.com/en-us/RebateStatus` accepts a cellular number or home address, enabling translation between phone numbers and physical addresses. Other rebate portals exist for Micro Center, Budweiser, Miller, Kohls, and various auto parts stores. Three Google searches help locate additional portals:

- `intitle:"rebate center"`
- `"track+my+rebate"`
- `"track+your+rebate"`

## Utility Inquiries

A target's telephone number and postal code may be enough to retrieve a full home address through a utility provider's troubleshooting portal. The CenturyLink example uses their internet troubleshooting page. Entering the telephone number and postal code returns the customer's name, full address, and telephone number. The author notes that many local utilities offer similar portals.

## Resume Searching

Resumes often contain a cellular number and personal email address that a subject would never post on a social network. Publicly available resumes are therefore high-value targets. Recommended Google, Bing, and Yandex query patterns for a named target include combinations of the target's name with the terms "Resume," "Curriculum Vitae," and "CV," applied with `filetype:doc`, `filetype:pdf`, and `site:docs.google.com` modifiers. Because some resume hosting services store resumes as images to frustrate text scrapers, these same queries should also be run through Google Images, which applies OCR to extract text from images. The Indeed resume section at `resumes.indeed.com` maintains a large collection. Contact information is typically redacted, but detailed work history, education, and location are often present.

## Supplementary Sources

**Ripoff Report** (ripoffreport.com) is a user-submitted complaint database for businesses and individuals. It is useful when a target engages with the public in a business capacity.

**Gift Registries** remain online long after the relevant event and contain personal details a subject would not post elsewhere. Useful data points include the partner's name, maiden name, wedding date and state (which can support a formal marriage certificate request), ceremony details and wedding party members (identifying close associates), planned travel and purchases, and due date and location from baby registries. The Knot can be searched with double asterisks as a wildcard first name to return all entries sharing a last name. Google site searches (e.g., `site:registry.thebump.com "target name"`) surface past registries that the host site no longer makes searchable. Key registry search pages:

- The Knot: `https://www.theknot.com/registry/couplesearch`
- The Bump: `https://registry.thebump.com/babyregistrysearch`
- Amazon Baby and Wedding registry search pages
- Walmart, Target, Ikea, Bed Bath and Beyond, Macy's, Registry Finder, and My Registry

**Find a Grave** (findagrave.com) and **People Legacy** (peoplelegacy.com, preferred since 2021) support locating proof of death and identifying living family members.

**Voter Registration** records are often public even when a person has unlisted phone numbers. The aggregator at `www.blackbookonline.info/USA-Voter-Records.aspx` and `voterrecords.com` link to county clerk databases for all fifty states, typically returning full name and address, sortable by name or address.

**Unclaimed Property** databases exist for every U.S. state and commonly require only a last name. Results nearly always include full name, home address, property type, and amount owed. The aggregator `unclaimed.org` provides access to all state sites.

**Google address searches** provide a final catch-all. Placing the street address in quotes and excluding the city (example: `"1234 Main" "Bethalto" IL`) surfaces remaining details about a target address.

## International Resources

For targets outside the United States the author lists:

- Australia: `www.peoplesearch.com.au`
- Canada: `www.canadapages.com`, `www.infobel.com/en/Canada`
- France: `www.infobel.com/en/France`
- Germany: `www.infobel.com/en/Germany`
- Spain: `www.infobel.com/en/Spain`
- UK: `www.192.com`, `www.peopletraceuk.com`, `www.gov.uk/government`

## Premium Option

Skopenow (Skopenow.com) is the one paid service the author recommends. It requires account creation and vetting. It returns current home addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, associates, and additional data that free services cannot match reliably.
