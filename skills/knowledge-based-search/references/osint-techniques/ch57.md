## Conclusion

The concluding chapter of OSINT Techniques is brief and forward-looking. The authors (Michael Bazzell and co-author Edison, signing as "MB & JE") close with several practical acknowledgments and a statement of philosophy about the field.

**On currency of the content.** The authors acknowledge that documenting OSINT techniques in print risks producing outdated material quickly. Technology changes fast and methods must adapt. They point readers to the IntelTechniques.com blog and the IntelTechniques Twitter/X account for ongoing updates, noting that new content is likely already posted by the time a reader reaches this page.

**Digital edition.** Since the original print release, the authors created an official digital PDF version of the eleventh edition. It is an exact replica of the print book, offered for those who want easy text copying, page printing, or full-color screen captures. Details are available at https://inteltechniques.com/book1.html.

**On the future of OSINT.** When asked about OSINT's future, the authors' answer is direct: the human investigator, not artificial intelligence or commercial "all-in-one" platforms, is the future. The authors decline advisory roles with companies building consolidated OSINT products because those easy solutions tend to be short-lived. Constant technology changes and automated data-collection restrictions erode the power of commercial tools over time. Truly valuable OSINT work requires manual effort from an individual analyst. The authors argue that OSINT analysis skills cannot be replaced by a machine, and they urge readers not to let any program become more capable than themselves.

**Community contribution.** Readers are encouraged to share discoveries with the community through Twitter or other networks: new resources found, new Linux application configurations in custom virtual machines, and workarounds when an online OSINT service disappears.

**Defensive OSINT.** The authors remind readers that every technique in the book can be turned on the investigator. When personal information is found exposed, action should be taken to protect it. They point to the companion series Extreme Privacy for readers who want to stay current on personal data defense.

**Acknowledgment.** The conclusion ends with thanks to the book's editors, crediting them with making the work coherent and giving them more credit than the closing note can fully express.
