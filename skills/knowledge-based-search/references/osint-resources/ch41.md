This unit covers the tail end of Chapter 24 (IP Addresses) and the opening section of Chapter 25 (Government and Business Records). It describes tools for locating networked devices, capturing a target's IP address, and searching public government and court records.

## Wireless Network Search: Wigle

Wigle.net (wigle.net) is a database of Wi-Fi access points collected by wardriving. Each entry can include the network SSID, router MAC address, encryption type, and GPS coordinates. Searching a username on Wigle may return results tied to home Wi-Fi networks the person named or registered. GPS coordinates from those results can be entered into Google Maps to identify a home address, satellite view, and street view. These results do not appear in standard search engines. A free account unlocks advanced search at wigle.net/search. Direct URL queries:

```
https://wigle.net/search?ssid=bazzell
https://wigle.net/search#fullSearch?postalCode=62002
```

## Shodan (shodan.io)

Shodan is a search engine for internet-connected devices rather than websites. It indexes device banners, which are metadata packets that devices send to clients, revealing server software, supported options, or welcome messages. Devices commonly found through Shodan include servers, routers, network storage, surveillance cameras, webcams, and VOIP systems. A free account is required for full filtering.

Shodan search filter syntax:

- `city:"San Diego"` (city name)
- `country:US` (two-letter country code)
- `geo:39.55,-111.45` (latitude and longitude)
- `os:Linux` (operating system)
- `net:18.7.7.0/24` (IP address range)
- `webcam` or `netcam` (keyword)

A city-name search such as `country:US city:"Mount Pleasant"` may return thousands of results and may match multiple cities sharing that name. Substituting a GPS coordinate such as `geo:39.55,-111.45` restricts results to the target area and reduces volume. Adding a keyword like `netcam` further filters to a specific device type. In the example given, `geo:39.55,-111.45 netcam` returned a single device in Mount Pleasant, Utah, identified as a Netcam on a Central Utah Telephone DSL connection.

Clicking an IP address in results connects to the device. Devices with no credential prompt can generally be accessed without a legal violation, though local laws may differ. Attempting to log in with default credentials such as "admin"/"admin" or "guest"/"guest" on a protected device may constitute computer intrusion.

Shodan Beta (beta.shodan.io) provides full IP detail through three URL patterns:

```
https://beta.shodan.io/host/70.39.81.131
https://beta.shodan.io/host/70.39.81.131/raw
https://beta.shodan.io/host/70.39.81.131/history
```

The history view shows all past scans with timestamps in the format `2020-04-26T07:49:17`. Shodan Maps (maps.shodan.io) supports location-based searches with a map interface. Shodan Images (images.shodan.io) displays webcam captures from open devices. Both are premium services requiring a fee.

## ZoomEye (zoomeye.org)

ZoomEye is a Shodan competitor that often returns unique results for the same queries. Direct URL query:

```
https://www.zoomeye.org/searchResult?q=70.39.110.82
```

## Threat Crowd (threatcrowd.org)

Threat Crowd searches IP addresses for associations with malware, malicious domain names, dates of discovery, and researcher comments. It is most relevant when investigating suspicious IPs. Direct URL query:

```
https://www.threatcrowd.org/ip.php?ip=70.39.110.82
```

## Censys (censys.io)

Censys is a search engine that queries a database built from daily internet scans. It shows how hosts and websites are configured, and can return details such as device type, ISP, and approximate location from an IP. Example: a search on 173.189.238.211 returned a Schneider Electric BMX P34 2020 device on a Windstream connection near Kansas City, Kansas. Direct URL query:

```
https://censys.io/ipv4/70.39.110.82
```

## IPv6 Addresses

IPv6 addresses, such as `2001:0db8:85a3:0000:0000:8a2e:0370:7334`, are increasingly common as IPv4 availability shrinks. Many standard OSINT utilities are adapting to accept them, but Bazzell recommends two dedicated IPv6 tools:

```
https://www.ultratools.com/tools/ipv6InfoResult?ipAddress=2001:db8::8a2e:370:7334
https://www.ultratools.com/tools/ping6?ipAddress=2001:db8::8a2e:370:7334
```

## Email Headers

Gmail and Yahoo do not disclose the sender's IP address in email headers. IP addresses in headers are primarily found when a target uses a desktop client such as Outlook to send from a business account. Two tools automate header analysis:

- IP2Location (ip2location.com/free/email-tracer): paste the full header text, receive the IP, location, ISP, and an interactive map.
- MX Toolbox (mxtoolbox.com/EmailHeaders.aspx): similar functionality.

## Capturing a Target's IP Address

The goal is to obtain the IP address assigned to a target by their ISP. This can verify approximate location, support a court order request, or determine whether multiple accounts share the same user.

### IP Logger (iplogger.org)

IP Logger creates tracking links that log the IP address, operating system, and browser of anyone who clicks them. The "URL and Image Shortener" service accepts any destination URL and generates a redirect link. Example output: `http://www.iplogger.org/3ySz.jpg`. Though it looks like a JPEG, clicking it forwards the target to the chosen destination while recording their connection details. A URL shortener such as Bitly (bit.ly) can make the link look less suspicious.

An image variant allows submission of an existing image URL. IP Logger generates both a direct link and an HTML `<img src="...">` tag that can be embedded in web pages or email messages. The image appears normally while collecting the same data.

Real-world example given: Bazzell was communicating with a hacker on a web forum. He embedded an IP Logger image link in a conversation thread by claiming to have a photo of a stolen debit card. The hacker loaded the image, and the log revealed an IP address in Newark, New Jersey.

### Canary Tokens (canarytokens.org)

Canary Tokens provides tracking options including PDF and DOCX files with embedded trackers. Opening one of these files alerts the creator to the opener's IP and general location. Bazzell considers it the most user-friendly of the public options and uses his own Canary Token documents as traps for people searching for his home address. VPNs and Tor can produce inaccurate or masked results.

### LinkBait (github.com/2YmlJesse/LinkBait, self-hosted)

Public IP logging services are well-known and may be blocked by email providers or recognized by sophisticated targets. Gmail typically blocks domains associated with IP Logger and Canary Tokens. LinkBait is a self-hosted PHP script created by a colleague of Bazzell. It runs on any Apache web server, including shared web hosts. Copy the PHP file to a web-accessible directory and rename it to `index.php`. Each visit writes a text file containing the date and IP address of the visitor.

Beyond the IP, the script collects an extended fingerprint including: browser and version, operating system, screen dimensions, GPU, installed fonts (used for browser fingerprinting), MTU (an MTU value other than 1500 suggests a VPN), active browser logins (for example, Google services), local router address, and system time and timezone. In the example shown, the script revealed that a visitor was using Firefox on macOS, connected through PIA VPN from a west coast server, with system time set to Mountain time, and logged into Google.

A non-logging test page is available at `https://inteltechniques.com/logger/` to see what data any website could collect from your own browser.

### GetNotify (getnotify.com)

GetNotify tracks when a target opens an email and captures their IP, location, and whether they read or immediately deleted the message. The service is free with a limit of five emails per day. Setup: create an account and register the sender address. To track, modify the recipient address by appending `.getnotify.com` after the full domain. For example, instead of `Michael@inteltechniques.com`, address the message to `Michael@inteltechniques.com.getnotify.com`. GetNotify routes the email to the real recipient and inserts an invisible tracking image. When the recipient opens the email, the image loads from a GetNotify server, capturing connection details and sending a notification to the sender. Log files are viewable in the GetNotify account dashboard.

## IntelTechniques IP Addresses Tool

The IntelTechniques custom tool aggregates the searches described above into a single interface. Entering an IP address and clicking "Populate All" fills that address into all search fields. "Submit All" opens browser tabs for all queries simultaneously. Searches covered include: reverse IP, locate IP, port scan, Whois, TraceRoute, reverse DNS, Bing IP, IPLocation, Wigle (SSID and postal), Shodan (standard, beta, raw, history), ZoomEye, Threat Crowd, Censys, ultratools IPv6 info and ping, and Dehashed.

## Government and Business Records (Chapter 25)

Open source government and business data has grown substantially due to government digitization efforts, lower digital storage costs, and data marketing. There is no uniform method across jurisdictions. County records, court records, and business registrations each vary by location and state.

### County General Records

Black Book Online (blackbookonline.info/USA-Counties.aspx) is a free directory that drills down from state to county and surfaces available online databases for each county. For Madison County, Illinois, available records included: coroner reports, delinquent tax sale, government expenditures, property tax search, public employee salaries, unclaimed property, crime map, building contractors, building permits, foreclosed properties, recorded documents, registered lobbyists, press releases, voter registration verification, and voter registration addresses.

Alternatives include Public Records Online (publicrecords.onlinesearches.com) and Netronline (publicrecords.netronline.com), which Bazzell describes as receiving more frequent updates.

### County Court Records

Black Book Online (blackbookonline.info/USA-County-Court-Records.aspx) also links to county court record databases. Example: St. Clair County, Illinois posts full civil and criminal court records online at co.st-clair.il.us/departments/circuit-clerk/courts. Searching a last name returns profiles with full name, date of birth, physical identifiers, case history, fines, pending appearances, and charged crimes including those that were dismissed. Madison County, Illinois records include: circuit court complete and attorney dockets, family and civil pro se dockets, felony jury trials, traffic and misdemeanor dockets, traffic citations, crash reports, police blotter, daily crime log, and jail inmate search. Some rural areas are not yet online.

### PACER (pacer.gov)

PACER (Public Access to Court Electronic Records) provides access to U.S. federal court documents including district courts, courts of appeals, and bankruptcy courts. It holds over 800 million documents. Cost is $0.10 per page, capped at $3.00 per document. The cap does not apply to name searches, non-case-specific reports, or transcripts. Fees are waived if usage stays under $15 per quarter. Account creation is free.

### RECAP (courtlistener.com/recap)

RECAP (PACER spelled backwards) is a browser extension for Firefox and Chrome. During a PACER search, it first checks the Internet Archive for a free copy of the document. If none exists and the user purchases the document from PACER, RECAP automatically uploads it to the Internet Archive for future free access. A standalone search interface is available at courtlistener.com/recap.

### UniCourt (unicourt.com)

UniCourt maintains a large court document database updated since 2018. Guests are limited to three free searches. Clearing the browser cache and switching VPN IP may reset this restriction. Full documents require a paid membership, but case summary pages are publicly accessible and indexed by Google. Searching within Google using `site:unicourt.com "target name"` often surfaces direct links to case pages. Free case summary data includes: case number, filing date, update date, status, type, jurisdiction, judge, courthouse, county, state, party names, and docket entries with dates and actions.

### Judy Records (judyrecords.com)

Judy Records is a free service with no subscription or business model. It currently indexes approximately 439 million U.S. court records. It supports standard search operators including quoted phrase search. Direct URL query:

```
https://www.judyrecords.com/getSearchResults/?search="michael bazzell"
```

### FOIA Search (foia.gov/search.html)

The Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) allows requests for government records. The foia.gov search interface uses Bing to index government websites and may surface documents that Google does not index. Bazzell found a specific 2001 committee record for "Darrell Bazzell" through this tool that could not be replicated in Google. Direct URL query:

```
https://search.foia.gov/search?affiliate=foia.gov&query=osint
```

### Open Corporates (opencorporates.com)

Open Corporates indexes business registrations for all 50 U.S. states and dozens of additional countries. Records include corporate officers' names, addresses, and contact details. It scrapes government sources directly and retains historical data that may no longer appear on the original government sites. Bazzell uses it whenever a business name or organization appears during an investigation. Supports query by business name, entity registration number, or officer name. Advanced address-based search requires a free account. Direct URL queries:

```
https://opencorporates.com/companies?q=inteltechniques
https://opencorporates.com/officers?q=bazzell
```

### AIHIT (aihitdata.com)

AIHIT uses artificial intelligence to populate and update business records. Bazzell notes he is uncertain how much of the AI characterization reflects genuine methodology versus marketing, but he consistently finds unique data there not available on Open Corporates. The text cuts off at the description of the default search option.
