## Telephone Number Searches (Chapter 18, continued)

### Format Variations on Craigslist and Search Engines

Craigslist posters often spell out phone numbers to avoid spam scrapers, writing "three one four five five five one two one two" instead of "314-555-1212". To cover multiple formats in one Google or Bing query, use the OR operator (`|`):

```
site:craigslist.org "314" | "three one four" "555" | "five five five" "1212" | "one two one two"
```

This catches combinations such as `314 555 one two one two`, `three one four 555-1212`, `314-555-1212`, and `3145551212`. It does not catch every variant (for example, "three fourteen" or "five fifty five twelve twelve"). The analyst should consider how the target might write the number creatively, and also try searching only the last four digits to cast a wider net, accepting more noise.

### Grocery Loyalty Card Lookup

Most U.S. grocery chains let customers substitute a cell number for a physical loyalty card at checkout. The cell number is stored with the customer's registered name. To exploit this: enter the target number at a POS terminal while making a small purchase (or at the store's fuel pump). The receipt or pump display will print the name on the registered account. The fictional number 847-867-5309 has been registered at grocery stores across the United States and returns a name at most chains.

### Escort Index (escortindex.com)

When a target number may be connected to sex work, drugs, or related criminal activity, search Escort Index. The site aggregates prostitution classifieds and review sites, extracts phone numbers from all online classified pages, and allows search by number. A single query can return online photos, escort ads, client reviews, ages used by the target, last known location, and locations derived from classified postings. Direct query URL pattern: `https://escortindex.com/search?search=<phone-number>`.

### Contact Exploitation via Android Emulator

When other methods fail, add the target number as a contact (named "John" or any name) in an Android virtual machine running in VirtualBox or Genymotion. Then open applicable apps one at a time (Twitter, Instagram, WhatsApp, Signal, Skype, dating apps) and use each app's "find my friends" or contact sync feature. The app will link any registered profile to the number in the contact list.

Real cases: in a 2019 child custody investigation, this method surfaced a dating profile with the husband's photo under a different name, and both numbers later appeared in Signal on the day divorce papers were served. A separate case identified a "burner" number owner through WhatsApp, which displayed a profile photo. Reverse image search on that photo located the subject's real social network profile.

### Calls.ai (sync.ai/calls-ai)

An Android app that queries a crowd-sourced contact database. Most results return only a first name, but accuracy is described as high. Steps: launch the Android VM, install Calls.ai from the Google Play Store or Aurora Store, allow default permissions, associate with the VM's Google account, and tap the search icon in the lower left to enter the target number. Truecaller operates similarly and should be used alongside Calls.ai.

### Data Breach Searches for Phone Numbers

Phone number searches in breach databases are generally unproductive but can be attempted at:
- `https://weleakinfo.to/`
- `https://breachdirectory.org/`

### IntelTechniques Telephone Tool

An automated tool that accepts area code, prefix, and last four digits and submits them across many services simultaneously. It includes:

- Caller ID API services (API keys required in the source code): EveryoneAPI, OpenCNAM, BulkCNAM, CallerIDService, AdvBackground, ServiceObjects, PeopleDataLabs, AmericaPhone, CallerSmart, CyberBackground.
- Aggregator lookup sites: Dehashed, FastPeople, InfoTracer, JohnDoe, Numpi, Nuwber, QKCaller, PeopleSearch, PhoneOwner, Reveal Name, Reverse Lookup, Searchpeople, Spytox, Sync.me, ThatsThem, TruePeople, USPhonebook, WhitePages, WhoseNo, YellowPages, ZabaSearch.
- Search engines: Google, Bing, Yandex, Craigslist.
- Historical phonebook records covering 1994 through 2003.
- Number-and-word variant queries across Google, Bing, and Craigslist.

## Online Maps (Chapter 19)

The central idea is to collect every available view of a target location, combining current and historical imagery from multiple services, because each service holds unique images taken at different times. All static URLs below use example coordinates latitude `41.947242`, longitude `-87.65673`. Replace those values with the target's coordinates, which are obtained by clicking any point on Google Maps or right-clicking and choosing "What's here".

### Google Maps (maps.google.com)

**Interface features:**

- Search bar accepts full addresses, partial addresses, GPS coordinates, business names, and business types.
- Satellite View: lower-left toggle. Provides a direct overhead view.
- Globe View: globe icon in the lower right. Clicking "3D" then the rotation icon shifts the perspective 45 degrees per click, revealing angled aerial views.
- Street View: drag the orange person icon (lower right) to any location with blue lines. Navigate by clicking forward, clicking and dragging, or scrolling. Double-clicking moves to that position.
- Historic Street View: while in Street View, click the small clock icon in the upper-left area to open a slider showing all stored captures for that location with month and year labels. Earlier images can reveal vehicles, structures, or conditions no longer visible.
- Distance measurement: right-click the starting point, choose "Measure distance", click to add path points, drag points to adjust. Total displays under the search box in miles and kilometers. Right-click to clear.
- GPS coordinates: click any point to see coordinates in the bottom-center window.

**Static URLs:**

### Zoom Earth (zoom.earth)

Aggregates imagery from NASA, Bing, and ArcGIS. The ArcGIS data is sometimes more current than Google or Bing. Full-screen mode allows clean captures without branding or borders, which is useful for live presentations. The second URL accesses historic imagery from the ESRI (Economic and Social Research Institute) database.

```
https://zoom.earth/#view=41.947242,-87.65673,20z
https://zoom.earth/#view=41.947242,-87.65673,20z/layers=esri
```

### HERE Maps (wego.here.com)

Originally a Nokia service with unique imagery. Now mostly mirrors Bing Maps, but occasionally holds unique views.

```
https://wego.here.com/?map=41.947242,-87.65673,20,satellite
```

### Yandex Maps (yandex.com/maps)

U.S. satellite imagery is often poor quality and low resolution. International locations tend to be clearer.

```
https://yandex.com/maps/?l=sat&ll=-87.65673%2C41.947242&z=20
```

### Descartes Labs (maps.descarteslabs.com)

Imagery sourced from NAIP (National Agriculture Imagery Program). Unique feature: image-similarity search. After locating a target, you can select a visual element (a monument, park, building, or field) and the service returns matching imagery from anywhere in the world. Investigative applications for this feature are not fully established, but it has clear potential.

```
https://search.descarteslabs.com/?layer=naip_v2_rgb_2014-2015#lat=41.94721&lng=-87.656502&skipTut=true&zoom=20
```

### Snapchat Map (map.snapchat.com)

Displays public Snapchat stories posted at any location. Less useful for residential addresses, more useful for specific events, schools, and public areas.

```
https://map.snapchat.com/@41.947210,-87.656502,20.00z
```

### Wikimapia (wikimapia.org)

Combines Google, Bing, and other satellite layers in one interface. Also holds crowd-sourced data: clicking any location or building presents a report of images, websites, and details contributed by users.

### Dual Maps (data.mashedworld.com/dualmaps/map.htm)

Displays Google Maps and Bing Maps satellite views side by side. Searching an address in the lower-left field centers both maps simultaneously. Useful for quickly identifying which service holds better imagery.

### Real Estate Sites (zillow.com, redfin.com)

Search a target address to find interior listing photos from when the property was for sale. These can reveal personal belongings, interests, and family information.

### Vidi (vidi.world)

A service that allows posting bounties for live video of a specific location. If a registered member in that area accepts, they record and send footage electronically.

## Historic Satellite Imagery

Mapping services continuously update imagery and usually display only the most recent capture. Earlier captures may show structures, vehicles, or conditions that have since changed.

### Historic Aerials (historicaerials.com)

Enter an address to browse aerial images across multiple decades. Quality degrades with older captures. All available years appear in the left panel.

### World Imagery Wayback (livingatlas.arcgis.com/wayback)

Preferred over Historic Aerials. Higher quality images, more captures per location, and a smoother timeline interface. Displays dated satellite snapshots of any location.

### Old Maps Online (oldmapsonline.org)

Road maps (not satellite imagery) covering approximately the past 100 years. Useful for identifying historical street names or determining when a road was built.

## Crowd-Sourced Street Views

Standard Google and Bing street views blur most faces and license plates. Crowd-sourced alternatives apply blurring inconsistently, which can expose information that official services suppress.

### Mapillary (mapillary.com)

Colored lines on the map indicate crowd-sourced street-level images uploaded by individuals, typically from GPS-enabled phones recording driving trips. Clicking a colored line opens the image sequence. The contributor's username and the capture date appear in the lower left.

License plate recovery: Mapillary applies blur boxes when a plate is judged clearly legible but often skips the box when the plate is farther away and harder to read. A partially visible plate can frequently be recovered by: cropping the image, inverting it in Photoshop, setting brightness to 0%, and setting contrast to 100%.

Static URL:
```
https://www.mapillary.com/app/?lat=41.947242&lng=-87.65673&z=18
```

In a 2020 missing person investigation, a Mapillary search revealed a vehicle parked outside the victim's residence that was unknown to investigators, opening a path that led to locating the victim.

### Karta (kartaview.org, formerly Open Street Cam)

Functions the same as Mapillary but holds unique imagery. Profile pages show the contributor's username, mapping history, image count, and profile photo. You can view all images from a specific contributor as a sequence of their travel.

```
https://kartaview.org/map/@41.947242,-87.65673,18z
```

### Land Viewer (eos.com/landviewer)

Weather satellite imagery at city-level zoom. Zoom resolution is too coarse to examine individual addresses. Primary use is documenting weather conditions (clouds, rain, clear skies) at a specific location on a specific date.

```
https://eos.com/landviewer/?lat=41.947242&lng=-87.65673&z=11
```

### Landsat Look (landsatlook.usgs.gov)

Three distinct historical satellite archives ranging from 1972 to the present, each accessible through a separate URL:

```
https://landlook.usgs.gov/viewer.html
https://landsatlook.usgs.gov/viewer.html
https://landsatlook.usgs.gov/sentinel2/viewer.html
```

Procedure: drag and zoom to the target location, select the desired satellite in the upper-right menu, choose a date range, and click "Show Images".

## Operational Application

Before executing a search warrant, investigators can combine satellite overhead views, four angled aerial views, historic imagery, and street-level views from multiple services to map entry points, obstructions, escape routes, and visible security cameras. The value of checking every mapping resource is demonstrated by cases where unique images on a secondary service (such as Mapillary) provided the key evidence that Google and Bing did not.
