## Overview

Chapter 13 covers OSINT techniques for social networks beyond Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram. The author's premise is that smaller and specialized networks often yield more intimate detail than the major platforms, because targets feel less exposed there. The chapter distinguishes between general social networks (LinkedIn, Snapchat) that attract a broad audience and niche online communities (TikTok, dating services) covered separately in Chapter 14. The core instruction throughout is to learn the technique rather than memorize specific resources, because URLs and services change over time while the underlying strategies persist.

## LinkedIn

LinkedIn has more than 675 million subscribers and is owned by Microsoft. It requires a free account to access most data. Because Microsoft also owns Bing, Bing often indexes LinkedIn content more thoroughly than Google does, and the chapter recommends using Bing as the primary search engine for LinkedIn queries.

### Search Filters and Direct URLs

The search bar at the top of any LinkedIn page accepts names, companies, locations, and titles. Results can be refined by category. Each category maps to a direct URL pattern:

- People: `https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/people/?keywords=john wilson`
- Posts: `https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/content/?keywords=john wilson`
- Companies: `https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/companies/?keywords=john wilson`
- Groups: `https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/groups/?keywords=john wilson`
- Jobs: `https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/index/?keywords=john%20wilson`
- Schools: `https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/schools/?keywords=john wilson`
- Events: `https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/events/?keywords=john wilson`

### Searching by Personal Details

The default `keywords` parameter in the People URL returns broad and often poor results for common names. Replacing `keywords` with structured parameters narrows results considerably:

- By first name only: `https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/people/?firstName=john`
- By full name: `https://www.linkedin.com/search/results/people/?firstName=john&lastName=smith`
- Adding employer: append `&company=microsoft`
- Adding title and school: append `&title=manager&school=Oklahoma`

Adding too many parameters can backfire if the target omitted some fields from their profile. When the URL approach fails, a Google site search fills the gap: `site:www.linkedin.com John smith Microsoft manager Oklahoma`.

### Profiles

LinkedIn profiles used for business networking often contain previous employment, alumni details, work associates, and sometimes phone numbers and email addresses. A direct username URL connects to a profile: `https://www.linkedin.com/in/ambermac/`.

### Posts

LinkedIn now functions as a full social network with posts, likes, and comments. The three-dot menu on any post exposes a static URL for that post, useful for evidence documentation. All comments should be expanded and truncated posts expanded with "...see more" before generating a screen capture. Clicking the clapping-hands count reveals a list of users who engaged with the post.

### Searching by Company

Clicking on a company page and then "See all employees on LinkedIn" returns redacted profiles showing only "LinkedIn Member" with job descriptions but no names. To unmask them, copy the visible job description (for example, "Account Executive at Uber") and run a Google site search: `site:linkedin.com "Account Executive at Uber"`. Browsing the Google Images tab for the same query often identifies the correct profile by photo faster than scanning text results. The "People also viewed" sidebar on any LinkedIn profile may expose the target with a full name. A reverse image search on the target's profile photo is another path to the unredacted page.

### Searching by Country

LinkedIn has country-specific subdomains that query the full network while emphasizing local users: uk.linkedin.com (UK), ca.linkedin.com (Canada), br.linkedin.com (Brazil).

### PDF Export

On any profile, clicking "More..." and then "Save to PDF" generates a document of publicly available information quickly.

### Search Engine Queries for LinkedIn

- Google: `site:www.linkedin.com john smith microsoft`
- Google Images: `https://www.google.com/search?q=site:linkedin.com+john+smith&tbm=isch`
- Google Videos: `https://www.google.com/search?q=site:linkedin.com+john+smith&tbm=vid`
- Bing: `site:linkedin.com john smith microsoft` (preferred, given Microsoft owns both Bing and LinkedIn)
- Yandex: `site:linkedin.com john smith microsoft`

### Viewing LinkedIn Without an Account

Every LinkedIn page redirects unauthenticated visitors to a login prompt. Google's Mobile Friendly Test (search.google.com/test/mobile-friendly) bypasses this. Submit any LinkedIn profile URL as the test input and the tool renders the mobile HTML of the profile without requiring login. For a more complete result: click the "HTML" tab on the test result page, click "Copy" above the source code, paste it into CodeBeautify (codebeautify.org/htmlviewer), and click "Run." The entire profile becomes visible because Google's servers have authorization to index LinkedIn without credentials.

### IntelTechniques LinkedIn Tool

The author's custom offline tool consolidates all the URL patterns above into a single interface accepting first name, last name, keyword, title, company, and school. It launches searches across LinkedIn directly, Google, Bing, and Yandex, and includes shortcuts for company search, group search, school search, event search, job search, username direct profile, mobile profile view, and photo searches.

## Snapchat

Snapchat is a difficult OSINT resource. Most content is private and auto-deletes. The chapter focuses only on web-based options, not the mobile app.

### Official Web Queries

- Keyword search: `https://story.snapchat.com/search?q=osint`
- Story by username: `https://story.snapchat.com/s/inteltechniques`
- User landing page: `https://www.snapchat.com/s/inteltechniques`
- Snap Map: map.snapchat.com shows public Stories pinned to geographic locations. Useful for monitoring a high-profile event at a specific place. The source code of any story page contains a static URL to the post.

### Third-Party Options

Third-party Snapchat search services index only a fraction of users, usually the most popular accounts, and appear and disappear frequently. Searching "Snapchat search users" is recommended to find current replacements. Two services mentioned at time of writing:

- SoVIP (sovip.io): supports filtering by user, location, age, and gender. Direct URL: `https://www.sovip.io/?pa=1&q=inteltechniques`
- Ghostdex (ghost.com / snapdex.com): accepts a username and may display bio, profile picture, location, and public snaps. Only useful if the target set a Snapchat username, which is optional.

Standard search engine site queries also apply: `site:snapchat.com "inteltechniques"` across Google, Bing, and Yandex.

## Google Networks

Every Google account has a numeric Google User ID that ties together Gmail, YouTube, Google Maps, photo albums, and other services. Finding this ID unlocks access to public contributions across those services.

### Finding a Google ID

Manual method: log in to a covert Google account, navigate to mail.google.com/chat, right-click and inspect the page, use "Find a Chat" to search the target's email address, then search the HTML source for `data-member-id="user/human/`. The long number after "human/" is the Google User ID.

Automated method: use Epieos (tools.epieos.com/cmail.php) to look up the ID from a Gmail address.

### Using the Google ID

Once the ID is known, two public endpoints expose further data:

- Photo albums: `https://get.google.com/albumarchive/[Google ID]`
- Map contributions: `https://www.google.com/maps/contrib/[Google ID]`

The map contributions page includes business reviews and location photos. The author demonstrates this using Justin Seitz (creator of Hunchly, who gave permission), whose Google ID is 100202552162672367520. His map reviews revealed interests and a likely area of residence. The technique can also confirm a target's connection to specific business reviews.
