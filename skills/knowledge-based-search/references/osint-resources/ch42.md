This chapter (Chapter 25 in the printed text, continuing into Chapter 26) covers publicly available online sources for government records, business data, vehicle information, criminal records, and virtual currency tracing. Each resource is presented with its URL structure so queries can be submitted directly without navigating menus.

## Business and Government Record Databases

**AIHIT** (aihitdata.com) accepts company names, registration numbers, email addresses, phone numbers, and individual names. The "More Fields" area enables reverse lookup from a personal email to associated businesses. Queries can be submitted via URL:

- `https://www.aihitdata.com/search/companies?k=email@gmail.com`
- `https://www.aihitdata.com/search/companies?t=Michael Bazzell`
- `https://www.aihitdata.com/search/companies?c=inteltechniques`

**OCCRP Aleph** (aleph.occrp.org) is a global archive of research material for investigative reporting. It contains business records, transcripts, and other data sets filterable by email address, phone number, name, and file type. Queries are free but results may be limited without a free account. URL format: `https://aleph.occrp.org/search?q=michael%20bazzell`

**Open Payrolls** (openpayrolls.com) holds nearly 85 million salary records from over 14,800 employers, covering federal agencies, states, counties, cities, universities, colleges, and K-12 schools. Direct query URL: `https://openpayrolls.com/search/michael-bazzell`

**US Staff** (bearsofficialsstore.com) holds millions of profiles tied to government and corporate employment, apparently scraped from LinkedIn. Searching a company name reveals all associated employees. Queries use a Google Custom Search Engine. The site was flagged as likely short-lived.

**Little Sis** (littlesis.org) is a grassroots watchdog network that maps connections between powerful people and organizations, including campaign donations, political support, board memberships, and other relationships.

**Bankruptll** (bankruptll.com) holds full court documents for millions of bankruptcy cases but has no useful free search interface. The recommended approach is a Google site search such as `site:bankruptll.com "bazzell"`. In the resulting URLs, adding `/text` to the end of a document path returns a plain-text extraction of the PDF. Removing `/text` returns the full PDF.

**Asset Locator** from Black Book Online (www.blackbookonline.info/assetsearch.aspx) is a directory of state-level sources for real estate, judgments, bankruptcies, tax liens, unclaimed funds, vehicle registrations, property tax records, and government salaries. Selecting an asset type and state generates a page of direct links to the relevant official databases.

## SSN and Death Records

**SSN Validator** (ssnvalidator.com) verifies whether a social security number is valid. It returns the issuing state, year of issue, confirmation of assignment, and a death flag if applicable. It does not reveal personal information tied to the number.

**Social Security Death Index** (genealogybank.com/explore/ssdi/all) requires only a first and last name. Results show birth year, death year, state of last residence, and state of SSN issue.

**Legacy** (legacy.com/search) indexes online obituaries and memorials from approximately 80 percent of all online newspapers. Results can identify family members and locations.

## Voter Registration

**Voter Records** (voterrecords.com) allows searching by name or browsing by state. Results include full name, home address, mailing address, gender, party affiliation, age, and relatives. Databases are available for Alaska, Arkansas, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Florida, Michigan, Nevada, North Carolina, Ohio, Oklahoma, Rhode Island, Utah, and Washington.

## Vehicle Research

### VIN Lookup

**DOT VPIC** (vpic.nhtsa.dot.gov) is the Department of Transportation's free VIN database, sourced directly from vehicle manufacturers. A VIN search returns year, make, model, trim level, body style, country of manufacture, and weight.

Additional free services accept a VIN and return year, make, and model. Vindecoderz (vindecoderz.com) often also shows estimated mileage from service records. Others include CarFax (carfax.com/vehicle-history-reports), Check That VIN (checkthatvin.com), Search Quarry (searchquarry.com), FaxVin (faxvin.com), Vehicle History (vehiclehistory.com), and VinCheck (vincheck.info).

**NICB VIN Check** (nicb.org/vincheck) adds two checks not available from standard decoders: theft records (VINCheck Theft Record) and salvage records (VINCheck Total Loss Records).

**Cycle VIN** (cyclevin.com) handles motorcycle VINs, which differ in character count from passenger vehicle VINs. Free use confirms year and make. A $25 fee retrieves title and mileage.

### License Plate Lookup

Free plate-to-VIN services include Auto Check (autocheck.com), Records Finder (recordsfinder.com/plate), CarFax, Search Quarry (searchquarry.com/vehicle_records), Free Background Search (freebackgroundcheck.org), Carvana (carvana.com/sellyourcar/getoffer/vehicle), VinCheck (vincheck.info/free-license-plate-lookup/), Vehicle History (vehiclehistory.com/license-plate-search), and Find By Plate (findbyplate.com). Results are hit-or-miss and rarely include an owner name, but they often return a VIN for further research. California plates return accurate data most often. States such as South Dakota and Montana share little registration data publicly.

**Kelley Blue Book** (kbb.com/instant-cash-offer) offers a free instant cash offer tool intended for sellers. Entering any license plate and its state returns the year, make, and model. Testing confirmed correct results for California plates and for plates in South Dakota and Wyoming, both states known to restrict registration data from public view. A test plate "MRB1G" in South Dakota returned a 2008 MAZDA MX-5 Miata (VIN JM1NC26FX80142016) and the same plate in Wyoming returned a 2019 Ram 2500 Crew Cab (VIN 3C6UR5DL7KG684611).

**O'Reilly Auto Parts** (oreillyauto.com) provides a "Shop by Vehicle" function in the upper-right corner. Entering a license plate and state returns year, make, model, and engine specifications. A California plate "HACKER" returned a 2007 GMC Sierra 1500 SLE with a V8 6.0L engine. A follow-up VinCheck search on the same plate returned VIN 3GTEK13Y87G527460.

**Progressive Insurance** (progressive.com) offers a free online quote form. Entering only the target's street address and zip code returns the year, make, and model of all vehicles registered at that address. No other accurate personal data is required beyond the physical address.

### Boats and Aircraft

**Marine Traffic** (marinetraffic.com) provides an interactive map showing the real-time location of all registered ships and boats globally. Clicking any vessel shows name, speed, collection time, and destination.

**Boat Info World** (boatinfoworld.com) accepts a boat name and returns owner, record date, registered address, hull ID, hailing port, Lloyd's Registry number, call sign, Coast Guard vessel ID, service type, length, gross tons, build year, shipbuilder, hull shape, and propulsion type.

**Plane Finder** (planefinder.net) displays a global map of all aircraft currently in flight. Hovering over a selection shows carrier, flight number, originating departure, destination, speed, and altitude. Tail numbers function like vehicle registration plates.

**Black Book Online Aviation** (www.blackbookonline.info/Aviation-Public-Records.aspx) provides direct links to aviation databases including Aircraft N-Number Search, Aircraft Ownership Search, Airline Certificates, Airport Profiles, Certified Pilots, Cockpit Voice Recorder Database, Flight Tracker, and Military Aviation Crash Reports.

## Campaign Contributions

All contributions to political campaigns are public record. Three sites allow searching by last name alone, with more detail when a full name and year are provided. Results include occupation, recipient, amount, contribution type, and a link to the official filing. Additional filters let you narrow by zip code, occupation, and year.

- Open Secrets (opensecrets.org)
- Money Line (politicalmoneyline.com)
- Melissa Data (melissadata.com/v2/lookups/fec/index), which accepts a zip code to return all donations for a specified year

## Additional Identity Records

**Selective Service Verification** (sss.gov/Home/Verification) requires last name, social security number, and date of birth. It returns full name, selective service number, and date of registration.

**High Programmer** (highprogrammer.com/cgi-bin/uniqueid) uses state-specific algorithms to predict a driver's license number from a person's name, sex, and date of birth. Supported states: Florida, Illinois, Maryland, Michigan, Minnesota, New Hampshire, New York, Washington, and Wisconsin.

**BinDB** (www.bindb.com/bin-database.html) takes the first six digits of any credit or debit card number (the Bank Identification Number) and returns the brand, issuing bank, card type, card level, country, bank website, and customer care number. A case example is provided: during a homicide investigation, a debit card used by the victim at a restaurant was run through BinDB to identify the issuing bank. Calling the bank's automated system with the card number and the victim's zip code returned the last 30 days of transactions. An unknown ATM withdrawal appeared on the day of the killing. ATM video showed a passenger in the victim's vehicle and opened a new investigative lead.

## Criminal and Inmate Information

**Family Watch Dog** (familywatchdog.us) searches sex offender registries by name (last name only is sufficient) or by address. Results include a photograph and offense details.

**Federal Inmate Locator** (www.bop.gov/inmateloc) requires first and last name. State prisons each maintain their own databases. Searching Google for "inmate locator" plus the state name surfaces the official state tool. Records typically include a photograph and details of the crime, and in most states remain publicly visible after release if the subject is on probation or parole.

**VINELink** (vinelink.com) is the online portal to VINE, the victim notification network. After selecting a state, users can search for offender custody status changes, offender court case dates, sex offender registry status, or protective order status. Registration allows automatic notification of changes.

A standing warning from the chapter: absence of online criminal records does not confirm no criminal history. These resources are secondary to a direct inquiry with courts or law enforcement.

## Police Radio Monitoring

**Broadcastify** (broadcastify.com) is the largest platform for live and archived streaming audio from public safety, aircraft, rail, and marine communications in the United States. Users browse feeds by state and county and listen to audio shared by scanner operators anywhere in the country. Every broadcast is recorded continuously. A paid membership ($30 per year) provides archive access, allowing replay of original dispatch and all follow-up radio traffic from any past event without a subpoena or FOIA request. The free tier allows limited streaming with advertisements.

**Radio Reference** (radioreference.com) is a database of radio frequencies for police, fire, and other emergency services, organized by state and county. Frequencies can be exported and programmed into a hardware scanner. The author uses a Uniden BCD396XT receiver programmed through ProScan (proscan.org) software pulling data from Radio Reference. A paid tier is also $30 per year. Radio Reference identifies frequencies and Broadcastify provides the audio stream, so the two services complement each other.

## Virtual Currency Tracing

### Address Lookup

**Blockchain** (blockchain.info) searches a Bitcoin address and returns the total number of transactions, total Bitcoin received (with USD value at time of query), current balance, and complete transaction history. Results are almost never linked to real names but provide full financial movement detail. The example address 12t9YDPgwueZ9NyMgw519p7AA8isjr6SMw had received 19.12688736 Bitcoin worth $287,391.14 at the time of writing. That address was used to collect ransom from malware victims.

**Bitcoin Who's Who** (bitcoinwhoswho.com) provides contextual analysis of a Bitcoin address. It flags known ransomware or malicious addresses, links to news coverage, and shows transaction IP addresses (often behind VPNs). The recommended workflow is to use Blockchain for transaction data and Bitcoin Who's Who for context.

**BlockChair** (blockchair.com) functions similarly to Blockchain but supports multiple currencies: Bitcoin, Ethereum, Ripple, Bitcoin Cash, Litecoin, Bitcoin SV, Dash, Dogecoin, and Groestlcoin. Each currency is queryable by URL, replacing `xxx` with the target address:

- `https://blockchair.com/bitcoin/address/xxx`
- `https://blockchair.com/ripple/address/xxx`
- `https://blockchair.com/bitcoin-cash/transaction/xxx`
- `https://blockchair.com/litecoin/address/xxx`
- `https://blockchair.com/bitcoin-sv/address/xxx`
- `https://blockchair.com/dash/address/xxx`
- `https://blockchair.com/dogecoin/address/xxx`
- `https://blockchair.com/groestlcoin/address/xxx`

**Bitcoin Abuse** (bitcoinabuse.com) checks whether a virtual currency address has been reported for malicious activity. It returns a summary of reported activity, activity type (ransomware, sextortion, etc.), and email addresses used in the scheme. Example: `https://www.bitcoinabuse.com/reports/1KUKcwCv64cXQZa4csaAlcF3PPTio6Yt2t`

**Wallet Explorer** (walletexplorer.com) clusters multiple Bitcoin addresses into a single wallet. Because a suspect may operate many addresses within one wallet, identifying the wallet reveals additional transactions not visible from a single address alone. Search the known address first, then follow the link to the wallet to expose all associated addresses:

- `https://www.walletexplorer.com/address/1EzwoHtiXB4iFwedPr49iywjZn2nnekhoj`
- `https://www.walletexplorer.com/wallet/00037fd441938ba4`

### Virtual Currency APIs

These URL-based APIs return plain text output and are suited for use in custom tools. Replace `xxx` with the target address or amount.

Validate an address (returns valid or invalid):
`http://codacoin.com/api/public.php?request=validate&address=xxx`

Current 24-hour Bitcoin price:
`https://blockchain.info/q/24hrprice`

Total Bitcoin received by an address. The result is in Satoshi (1 BTC = 100,000,000 Satoshi). Satoshi is used because at approximately $19,000 per Bitcoin, fractional values are more precise in Satoshi.
`https://blockchain.info/q/getreceivedbyaddress/xxx`

Total Bitcoin sent by an address (also in Satoshi):
`https://blockchain.info/q/getsentbyaddress/xxx`

Current balance of an address (in Satoshi):
`https://blockchain.info/q/addressbalance/xxx`

Convert a Bitcoin amount to USD. Replace `xxx` with the Bitcoin value:
`http://codacoin.com/api/public.php?request=convert&type=btctofiat&input=xxx&symbol=enabled&decimal=2&exchange=average&currency=USD&denom=bitcoin`

Convert a USD amount to Bitcoin. Replace `xxx` with the USD value:
`http://codacoin.com/api/public.php?request=convert&type=fiattobtc&input=xxx&symbol=enabled&decimal=10&exchange=average...`

## IntelTechniques Business and Government Tool

The IntelTechniques custom web tool bundles many of the searches above into a single interface. It accepts a name or company name and covers PACER, RECAP, UniCourt, JudyRecords, FOIA, OpenCorporates, AIHIT, Open Payrolls, US Staff, LittleSis, Open Secrets, MoneyLine, and Voter Records. A separate panel handles email and telephone lookups via AIHIT, SSN verification via the Social Security Death Index, and virtual currency checks.
